how the ai coach works

not a template engine

Syntax's AI coach is a conversational AI that reads your actual training history — every set, every exercise, every session. It understands your available equipment, your recent volume, and your goals. When it writes a program, it writes one that is genuinely yours.

how it works

  • You tell it what you need in natural language — "give me a leg day for 30 minutes" or "upper body push, I only have dumbbells today"
  • It reads your workout history and knows your equipment
  • It writes a structured program in markdown — warmup sets, working sets, RPE targets, rest guidance
  • You tap to start training immediately

coach memory

The AI coach remembers you across conversations. Your profile, training preferences, injuries, goals, and patterns are stored in a memory.md file on your phone — plain text, fully editable.

The coach learns your context over time so you don't have to repeat yourself. And because it's a markdown file, you can read, edit, or delete anything it remembers.

memory.md — fully editable

your data stays private

AI coaching does not mean cloud storage. When you send a message, it is routed through a secure proxy to the AI provider. Nothing is stored on our servers. Your workout data never leaves your device — the AI sees only what you send in that conversation.
